METROHUB WIDGET IDEAS
=====================

1. Live Hardware Pulse (CPU / GPU / RAM)
- Shows a glowing heartbeat/wave line that moves with your CPU, GPU, and RAM usage.
- Click or hover to see which apps are using the most memory.
- Quick button to close heavy apps or clean up RAM with one click.

2. Ambient Sounds & Lo-Fi Noise
- Play relax and focus sounds right from the widget without opening a browser.
- Pick and mix sounds: Rain, Thunder, Campfire, White/Brown noise.
- Sliders for volume.
- Automatically pauses when you play music on your media player.

3. Desktop Pixel Pet / Companion
- A cute pixel art pet (cat, mini robot, or ghost) that lives on your desktop tile.
- Dances with headphones on when music is playing.
- Sleeps when you are away from your PC.
- Types on a tiny laptop when you are busy typing.
- Click to pet or interact with it.

4. Smart Clipboard & Color Stash
- Shows your recent copied text, links, and photos.
- If you copy a color code (like #FF0055), it shows the actual color box on the tile.
- Lets you pin important text or links so you can copy them again with one click.

5. World Clock & Sun Tracker
- Shows a small world map with a live daylight/night shadow moving across the earth.
- Add cities like Tokyo, London, New York.
- Shows if it is day, night, sunrise, or sunset in those cities.

6. Quick Command & Shortcut Buttons
- One-click buttons on your desktop to do fast tasks:
  * Flush DNS
  * Restart Windows Explorer
  * Switch Windows Dark / Light mode
  * Safely remove USB drives
  * Open favorite folders or apps

7. Flip News Ticker (Hacker News / Reddit)
- A tile that flips over every few seconds like classic Windows Phone tiles.
- Shows top news headlines from tech sites, gaming, or Reddit.
- Click any headline to open it in your browser.
