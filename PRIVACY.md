# Privacy Policy for MetroHub Companion

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

MetroHub Companion is a lightweight browser extension designed to send web links from your browser to your local MetroHub desktop launcher canvas.

### Data Collection & Usage
- **Local Operation Only:** MetroHub Companion accesses the active tab's URL and title only when you explicitly click the extension popup or press the keyboard shortcut (`Alt+M`).
- **No External Transmission:** All data is sent strictly to your local machine (`http://127.0.0.1`) where your MetroHub desktop application is running. No data is ever transmitted to external cloud servers, third parties, or developers.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** The extension does not track browsing history, does not use cookies, does not collect personal identifiers, and contains no tracking, telemetry, advertising, or analytics code.
- **No Data Retention:** The extension does not store your browsing data. User settings (such as chosen tile colors and default groups) are stored locally on your device via standard browser storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local`).

### Third Parties
MetroHub Companion does not share, sell, or monetize any user data with any third party under any circumstances.
